# Contributing to MineUX

Thanks for wanting to help. MineUX is not open source: its code is published for reading, and
all rights are reserved (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)). Contributing is entirely voluntary, and
MineUX owes contributors no credit, payment or anything else for what they send.

Because the owner may also offer MineUX under other terms (including commercial or
closed-source ones), every contribution comes in under the **MineUX Contributor Agreement**
below. Nothing is merged until its author has accepted it.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a small fix, so nobody's work is wasted.
2. Keep a pull request to one change, with a clear description and the tests it needs
   (`./gradlew build` runs them, with the source checks).
3. Accept the Contributor Agreement (next section) before the pull request can be merged.
4. Don't include code you didn't write unless its licence allows it and you say where it came
   from in the pull request (see 5.4 of the agreement).

## How acceptance is recorded

- **On GitHub (the normal way):** a CLA bot (CLA Assistant) comments on your first pull request
  with a link to this agreement. You accept it once with your GitHub account; the bot records
  your account, the agreement version and the date, and marks the pull request as signed. That
  acceptance covers all your later contributions under the same version.
- **Without the bot** (no bot set up yet, or a contribution sent another way): post this
  sentence yourself, from your own account, on your first pull request or with your first patch:

  > I have read the MineUX Contributor Agreement 1.0 and I agree to it for this contribution and
  > all my future contributions to MineUX.

  and end every commit message with a sign-off line in your real name and email address:

  ```
  Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
  ```

  (`git commit -s` adds it.) The maintainer records the sentence (link, account, date,
  agreement version) before merging. A sign-off line alone is **not** acceptance; it confirms
  each commit is covered by the agreement you already accepted.
- **Contributing for an employer or other organisation:** email support@mineux.com before
  your first contribution. The organisation has to agree too (see 5.2).

If the agreement changes, it gets a new version number, and contributions after that need the
new version accepted. Contributions already made stay under the version that was in force
when they were made.
